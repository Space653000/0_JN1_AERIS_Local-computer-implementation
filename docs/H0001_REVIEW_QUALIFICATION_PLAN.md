## Noncircular qualification

Reuse the sealed Role Acceptance execution engine to test each **review Skill**
against manually authored candidate results and expected review decisions. Cases
must include positive, boundary, negative and counter-hypothesis decisions, wrong
units, incorrect result/decision, unsupported calibration/physical claims and
out-of-scope context. Expected values/decisions must not be generated by the
executor, review implementation, or shared Skill fixtures.

The review implementation resides separately from the executor calculations.
It may share input-schema validation, hashing and basic arithmetic, but may not
invoke the executor to manufacture its verdict. Numerical crosschecks alone do
not suffice: failed constraints, proposed disposition, counter-hypothesis and
next experiment must agree. Review inputs come from sealed executor Evidence.

Current intact role-specific execution Evidence for this explicitly review-kind
Skill establishes the *bounded review capability*. It does not require prior L3
and does not award L3. Bind qualification to role ID, contract, review Method,
review suite, implementation and policy hashes. A tampered, stale, swapped or
missing qualification is ineligible. Human credentials remain unclaimed.

## Router and workflow integration

Router requires all review capabilities for the requested execution Skill, plus
product/transducer/lifecycle/risk/evidence-class applicability. No keyword-only
selection, runtime reviewer override or fallback to R098. Exclude execution and
conflicted seats; choose deterministic qualified candidates, never inferred
expertise from a role name or maturity label. Record all selection reasons,
uncovered review domains and qualification Evidence references.

For speaker power require both nonlinear and thermal reviews; for the bounded
TWS calculation require both ANC and fit/capture reviews. These requirements do
not assert that the present TWS suite covers manufacturing or full product life.

After real workflow execution and sealing, reconstruct review input from the
bundle, run the selected review Skills with fresh per-review input context, and
seal independent review records bound to the executor run and qualification.
Any discrepancy produces CHANGES_REQUIRED with consequential next action.
Aggregate success means only the stated bounded software review passed. Store
the review and disagreement/lesson sources in append-only Memory. Do not promote
Memory into Evidence, rewrite executor Evidence or award role L3 automatically.
