# speaker-digital-transport-baseline

Use only for a declared serial-audio transport contract. Check word/slot fit,
BCLK and frame-sync arithmetic, active-slot mapping, worst-case service margin
and buffer latency. This does not measure electrical timing, execute a driver,
prove glitch-free playback, award role-wide L3 or replace Human approval.
