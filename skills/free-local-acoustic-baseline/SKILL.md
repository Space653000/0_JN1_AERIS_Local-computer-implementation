# Free Local Acoustic Analysis Baseline

Deterministic, dependency-free local signal-analysis adapter for FFT, frequency response,
THD, SNR, linear SPL calibration, impulse response, octave / third-octave aggregation,
one-pole filtering, transfer function, STFT, coherence, SVG plotting, and evidence-ready
JSON reporting.

Truth boundary: `FREE_BASELINE` is not `LICENSED_PROFESSIONAL_VERIFIED`. Traceable
calibration, instrument correlation, physical DUT evidence, and formal engineering
acceptance remain separate gates.
