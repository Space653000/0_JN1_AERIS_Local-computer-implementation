# speaker-signal-chain-noise-headroom-baseline

Use only with declared positive scalar RMS voltage gains, bandwidth-integrated
uncorrelated input-noise terms, source/load impedances and peak capability. Check
referred noise, source loading, peak voltage/current, minimum load and supplied
phase margin without confusing a nominal resistive load with complex stability.
The result is not bench stability, spectral-noise, rail-transient, calibration,
role-wide L3 or Human acceptance.
