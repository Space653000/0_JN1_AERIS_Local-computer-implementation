# next-experiment-safety-screening-baseline

Use only for a declared proposed next-experiment point, a declared safe
region, and the already-computed distance from that point to its nearest
previously tested point. Checks whether the point falls inside the declared
safe region and represents sufficiently new information relative to prior
coverage. This does not search or maintain a point-history itself, does not
override any risk gate, and does not authorize physical execution.
