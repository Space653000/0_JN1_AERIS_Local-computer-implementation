# vr-xr-headset-screening-baseline

Use only for a declared motion-to-sound latency, declared strap-rubbing
noise level and declared speech reference level, against declared
acceptance thresholds. Checks the latency against its bound and the
rubbing-to-speech margin (in dB) against its minimum. This does not measure
a pose-prediction or physical-fit distribution across users, does not
execute a physical wear test, and does not authorize Human production
sign-off.
