# humanoid-interaction-screening-baseline

Use only for a declared residual self-echo level, user interruption level,
joint noise level during head-tracking motion, user speech reference level
and declared acceptance thresholds. Checks the interruption-to-residual-
echo margin against its minimum and the joint-noise-to-speech margin
against its minimum. This does not cover a full conversational-turn or
head-tracking-motion coverage matrix, does not execute a physical
instrument measurement, and does not authorize Human production sign-off.
