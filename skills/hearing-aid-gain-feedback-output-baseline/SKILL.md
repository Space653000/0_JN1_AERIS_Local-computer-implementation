# Hearing Aid Gain / Feedback / Output Baseline

R045 uses this deterministic local method to keep prescribed insertion gain,
vent loss, feedback onset, MPO and receiver headroom as separate decisions.

It accepts exact declared-unit synthetic or user-supplied parameters and emits
bounded checks, revisions, counter-hypotheses and unresolved clinical work.
It never certifies patient fitting, medical benefit, real-ear measurement,
physical safety, L3/L4 maturity or Human approval.
