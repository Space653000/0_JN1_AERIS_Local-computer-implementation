# speaker-polar-spatial-baseline

Use only with symmetric, strictly ordered horizontal angles and a supplied
absolute on-axis reference. Check coverage, largest angular gap, uncertainty-
bounded edge attenuation, left/right symmetry and alignment uncertainty. Do not
peak-normalize away absolute loss. The result covers discrete supplied samples,
not continuous lobes, vertical/full-sphere directivity, causal attribution,
calibration, role-wide L3 or Human approval.
