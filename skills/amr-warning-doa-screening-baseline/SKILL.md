# amr-warning-doa-screening-baseline

Use only for a declared warning level, drive-motor noise level, robot
velocity, DOA update interval and declared acceptance thresholds. Checks
the warning-to-motor-noise margin against its minimum, and the robot's
displacement during one DOA update interval against a declared position-
uncertainty bound. This does not cover a full velocity/load coverage
matrix, does not execute a physical instrument measurement, and does not
authorize Human production sign-off.
