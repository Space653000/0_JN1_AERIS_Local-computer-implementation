# speaker-filter-realization-baseline

Use for supplied normalized real biquads and declared crossover, coefficient,
peak and delay bounds. Check pole radii, quantization, one crossover sum/phase,
peak headroom and group delay. This does not prove full-band fixed-point
behavior, physical playback, role-wide L3 or Human approval.
