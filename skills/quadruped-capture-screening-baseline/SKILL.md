# quadruped-capture-screening-baseline

Use only for a declared footfall peak level, capture headroom, body
orientation and declared acceptance thresholds. Checks the footfall
headroom margin against its minimum and the body orientation against a
declared fixed-beam tolerance. This does not cover a full gait-mode/
orientation coverage matrix, does not execute a physical instrument
measurement, and does not authorize Human production sign-off.
