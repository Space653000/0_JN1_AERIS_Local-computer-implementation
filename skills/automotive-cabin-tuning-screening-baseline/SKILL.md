# automotive-cabin-tuning-screening-baseline

Use only for a declared driver-seat and rear-seat level, a declared
acceptable spread, and a declared road-noise-validation flag. Checks that
the driver/rear seat level spread stays within the declared bound and that
the tuning was validated under road-noise conditions rather than only in a
parked cabin. This does not cover a full seat-position/vehicle-speed
coverage matrix, does not execute a physical in-cabin measurement, and does
not authorize Human production sign-off.
